## 🎯 Executive Summary: Why This Works
The global success of **Wordle** (acquired for \$1M+), **Murdle** (#1 New York Times Bestseller), **Connections**, and **GeoGuessr** proved that human beings crave **frictionless daily mental micro-breaks**. 

When people are bored at school, work, or before bed:
1. They don't want a 2-hour tutorial or a 5GB download.
2. They want **instant agency, mystery, and a quick dopamine reward**.
3. They love **sharing their score without spoiling the answer** to show off their deductive intellect.

### Pillar 2: The Amazon KDP Paperback Books (The Murdle Multi-Million Strategy)
* **The Reality:** G.T. Karber built *Murdle* as a website first, then compiled 100 cases into a paperback puzzle book sold on Amazon. It became the **#1 Bestseller across all books on Amazon UK & US**.
* **How to Execute:**
  1. Use the included `scripts/case_generator.py` script to generate 100 unique, mathematically validated mystery puzzles.
  2. Click the **"Print Kit"** button in Sleuth or format the output into a 6"x9" book layout using Canva or LaTeX.
  3. Upload the PDF to **Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing)** as a paperback.
  4. Cost to you: **\$0 upfront**. Amazon prints and ships on demand, paying you **\$4 to \$7 royalty per book sold**.
  5. Sell just 15 copies/day = **\$2,250 – \$3,150/month passive income**.

### Pillar 3: "Sponsor a Crime Scene" & Coffee Tip Jar

## 🚀 The 0-to-100k Audience Growth Playbook (Zero-Ad Spend)

### 1. The TikTok & Instagram Reels "BookTok" Hack
True crime and mystery enthusiasts are one of the most active subcultures on social media:
* **The Video Format (15–25 seconds):**
  * **Hook (0-3s, text on screen):** *"If you can solve this 2-minute murder mystery, you're in the top 1% of detectives."*
  * **Middle (3-15s):** Screen recording of filling in the Sleuth logic grid while moody noir jazz or a trending mysterious sound plays. Show the clues: *"Dr. Finch was seen in the Library... but who held the candlestick?"*
  * **End (15-20s):** Don't show the answer! End with: *"I made my accusation and got it in 1m 45s. Can you beat my time? Link in bio."*
* **Hashtags to Use:** `#murdle #puzzletok #wordle #brainteaser #riddles #logicpuzzle #mysterytok #bored`

### 2. The Reddit Community Flywheel
Post high-value, non-spammy showcases in communities that love deduction:
* **Target Subreddits:**
  * `r/puzzles` (500k+ members)
  * `r/webgames` (700k+ members)
  * `r/detective` and `r/mystery`
  * `r/SideProject` and `r/IndieHackers`
* **Post Title Formula:**
  > *"I was bored so I spent the last week coding a 2-minute daily murder mystery game with logic grids and typewriter sound effects. It's 100% free, no login required."*
* **Why it converts:** Redditors love clean, no-login, indie-made passion projects that respect their time.

### 3. The "Show HN" (Hacker News) Launch
* Post a `Show HN: Sleuth – A daily 2-minute deduction puzzle with procedural logic grids`.
* Highlight the procedural generation script, the Web Audio synthesizer, and the zero-friction experience. Hacker News frequently drives 30,000–80,000 tech professionals in 48 hours.

### 4. Product Hunt Launch Day
* Prepare 5 high-resolution GIFs showing the logic grid filling up, the arrest warrant stamp slamming down, and confetti flying.
* Tagline: *"A daily 2-minute noir murder mystery to cure your boredom."*
